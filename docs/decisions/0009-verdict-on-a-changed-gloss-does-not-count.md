# 0009 — A verdict on a gloss that has since changed does not count as reviewed

**Date:** not dated in the handover note; applied on 2026-09-27 (commit `5acb170`)
**Status:** accepted

## What was decided

Where the reviewer gave a verdict on gloss text that was regenerated afterwards, the
row does not count as reviewed. That is why the reviewed total is 250 and not the
257 verdicts the file holds. Separately, where she supplied a Portuguese example
sentence without an English translation, the English is written by us and the row's
note says so.

## Why

A verdict is only evidence about the text it was given on. Counting the other seven
would overstate how much of the list a native speaker has actually checked.

## What it costs

Seven rows have to be shown to her again, and the headline review figure is smaller
than the raw count. The thirteen English translations written on her behalf are ours
rather than hers, so they need confirming on the next pass.
