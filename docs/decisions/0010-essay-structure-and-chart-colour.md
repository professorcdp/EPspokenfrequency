# 0010 — Essay structure, and orange used for marks but never for text

**Date:** not dated in the handover note
**Status:** accepted

## What was decided

The Substack essay opens with the motivation — the "ten words you need" videos,
corpus linguistics, and Davies as the gold standard — and only then gives the
finding. The charts are restyled for Substack, and the Substack orange `#FF6719` is
used for chart marks only, never for text.

## Why

The finding means little to a reader who does not yet know why anyone would count
words. The orange fails contrast requirements as text, so it is kept to shapes where
contrast rules do not apply in the same way.

## What it costs

The essay takes longer to reach its point, which costs readers who skim. Restyled
charts also have to be regenerated if the comparison numbers change.
