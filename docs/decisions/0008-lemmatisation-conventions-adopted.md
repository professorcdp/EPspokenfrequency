# 0008 — The lemmatisation conventions in eval/conventions.md are adopted

**Date:** not dated in the handover note; the file was added on 2026-09-17
**Status:** accepted

## What was decided

The five conventions written up in `eval/conventions.md` govern how words are
grouped: contractions are their own entries, adjectives fold to the masculine but
person nouns keep the feminine, diminutives stay separate, comparatives and
superlatives stay separate, and pre-1990 spellings merge under the post-1990 form.

## Why

Adopted on the advisor's recommendation — Jim's words were "I'll go with your
recommendations". Each convention is a judgement call with no automatic test, so
writing them down was the only way to apply them consistently.

## What it costs

Some of the calls are arguable and visible in the published list: `menina` and
`menino` are separate entries while `alto` and `alta` are one. Changing a convention
later moves counts and ranks, and would change Anki note identity for the affected
words.
