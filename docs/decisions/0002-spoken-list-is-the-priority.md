# 0002 — The spoken list is the priority; Davies is only for comparison

**Date:** not dated in the handover note, which lists the decisions in roughly
chronological order
**Status:** accepted

## What was decided

This repository's spoken frequency list is the deliverable. The extraction of Mark
Davies's frequency dictionary is kept only so the two can be compared, and lives in
a separate private repository.

## Why

The comparison is evidence for the list, not a product in its own right, and the
Davies material cannot be redistributed.

## What it costs

The comparison work cannot be shown in public in full — only aggregate numbers and
a handful of ranks may be quoted — so the essay that draws on it has to be written
carefully and cannot link to its own sources.
