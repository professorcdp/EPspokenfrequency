# 0006 — The native-speaker reviewer stays anonymous

**Date:** not dated in the handover note
**Status:** accepted

## What was decided

The Portuguese teacher who reviews the glosses and labelled the *ser*/*ir* sample is
credited only as "a native-speaker Portuguese teacher", with no name, anywhere in
the repository or in public writing.

## Why

It was her preference, and the project has no need of her name to be credible.

## Unsettled

The handover note says she was offered named credit on the gloss review and has not
asked for it, but marks this **(unsure)** — the advisor could not confirm it from
the files. Treat it as unverified: if named credit matters later, ask her again
rather than assuming the offer was made.

## What it costs

A real contribution goes uncredited by name, and anyone judging the review's
quality has to take the description on trust.
