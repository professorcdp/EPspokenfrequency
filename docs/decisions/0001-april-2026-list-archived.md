# 0001 — The April 2026 list is archived, not supported

**Date:** mid-September 2026 (the only date the handover note gives)
**Status:** accepted

## What was decided

The earlier April 2026 word list is archived at the tag `v0-april-2026` and carries
no compatibility obligations. It is referred to only lightly in writing ("before
Fable came out").

## Why

It was never publicised, so nobody is relying on it. More generally, Jim would
rather wait for a complete, correct version than release something early.

## What it costs

Anyone who did happen to take a copy gets no upgrade path, and the ranks between
the two lists cannot be compared without re-deriving them. Waiting for the right
version also means the list sat unreleased for months.
